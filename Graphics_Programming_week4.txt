1. Ray optics describes light as rays traveling through space.

Reflection ---> Light bounces off surface
Refraction ---> Light changes direction when passing between 			different media
Transmission -> Light passes through a material

2. Wave optics describes light as a wave
It explains:
- Diffraction: Light spreads or bends around obstacles and through small openings.
- Interference: Light waves combine and can reinforce or cancel each other.

3. Electromagnetic Optics describes light as an electromagnetic wave.

It explains:
- Polarization: The orientation of light's electric-field oscillators
- Dispersion: Different wavelengths of light travel at different speeds in a material.

4. Quauntum Optics describes light at the quantum level.
It explains:
- Fluorescence: A material absorbs energy and emits light relatively quickly
- Phosphorescence: A material can continue emitting light after excitation stops.

Stationary charge:
- Produces an electic field 
- Does not produce a magnetic field in its rest frame

Moving charge:
- Produces an electric field 
- Also produces a magnetic field relative to the observer


The electic field of a moving charge differs from that of a stationary charge, as described by special relativity.

| Electric Field                                                           | Magnetic Field                                              |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Produced by electric charges                                             | Produced by moving charges                                  |

| Can exert force on stationary charges                                    | Magnetic force acts on moving charges                       |
