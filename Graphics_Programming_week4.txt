It explains:
- Polarization: The orientation of light's electric-field oscillators
- Dispersion: Different wavelengths of light travel at different speeds in a material.

It explains:
- Fluorescence: A material absorbs energy and emits light relatively quickly
- Phosphorescence: A material can continue emitting light after excitation stops.
